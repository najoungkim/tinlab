**tinlab** studies humans and machines, centered around topics like (linguistic) generalization, meaning, evaluation methods and design, and the nature of computation and representation that underlies language and cognition. tinlab members come from a wide range of academic backgrounds including Linguistics, Cognitive Science, Computer Science, Data Science, and Psychology.

tinlab is led by [Najoung Kim](https://najoung.kim), and is affiliated with the Department of [Linguistics](https://www.bu.edu/linguistics/), [Computer Science](https://www.bu.edu/cs/), and [Computing & Data Science](https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/) at Boston University. The lab is also a part of the SALAMI Consortium, a growing group of researchers at BU interested in computation and language.
